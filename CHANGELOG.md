# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First public release.

- Slide: throw Zen's mini player into a corner with a click-drag or a two-finger flick; it slams into the screen edge and slides into the corner. Slow drops stay put. Throws stay on the current screen.
- Arc-style controls: Back to Tab, Minimize, Close, play/pause, ±15 s, scrubbable timeline with upward-drag fine seeking, sound on/off button synced with Zen's sidebar media controls, right-click Mute / Copy URL.
- Pop out any playing video on tab switch: muted, short, silent, or paused by the page when you leave the tab (Instagram and similar).
- macOS installer, one-line `curl` installer, and uninstaller.
